About us

A studio built on community

In Motion Dance is a family-run studio that has been operating for fourteen years and has grown to around 130 students across children's and adult programs.

The teaching team includes five teachers, including two family members and three former students who returned after completing their training. That continuity reflects the welcoming, community-focused experience the studio aims to create.

We want students to enjoy dance, build confidence and feel part of a supportive community.
